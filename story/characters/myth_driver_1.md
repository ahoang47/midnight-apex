- **Role:** Antagonist / Former ally
- **District:** Touge
- **Personality (locked 2026-11-04):**

  - Sure he's right: insists she left on her own.
  - Loyal to the old code: respects driving skill (why he spots Sabine's work on the PC's car), but won't trust her because of her family.
