# PROLOGUE

## 1. Opening Sequence

- PC (mid-20s, any gender) moves to the city for work — really running from something: walked out on their hometown crew without explaining why: the PC was its driver and left the week of a big race for a job in the city, so the crew thinks the PC ditched them for something better; really, the PC felt like the weak link holding them back
- PC personality (locked 2026-11-04, kept light so players can project): deflects about themselves (dodges "where you from?"); quick to decide what other people think (assumed the crew saw them as the weak link, assumes Sabine is the snitch: the same mistake the Myth Drivers made); earnest and driven once locked on
- Arrives at dusk; night from then on
- Nighttime exploration; first exposure to underground racing culture
- Overworld tutorial on the drive home from work at dusk
- On a back road, a car flashes its headlights: first race challenge (hazards to accept)
- First race (battle tutorial); PC ignores a manifold warning and the engine blows
- The challenger (Sabine) comes back; PC deflects her questions about where they’re from; she tows the car to her shop
- Why she takes PC in: on the surface, she challenged PC on the back road, feels personally responsible for the blown motor, and fronts the rebuild on a repair tab; underneath, she sees herself in PC — someone who showed up alone and won’t say why (she never admits it)

## 2. Garage Intro

- Sabine introduces Jesse and Reiko at the garage; they size up PC as an outsider
- Tuner, Jesse (a nod to *The Fast and the Furious*): warm and easygoing, welcomes the PC right away; mods, installs a DAQ system — more races, more data, better tuning. The DAQ also keeps the last race for playback, **the DAQ log** (canon 29): Jesse hooks it up after opening race 2. Works out of Sabine's garage; not Sabine's employee, he just hangs out with her (canon 24). **Arc (first pass 2026-11-05, needs more passes):** conflict-averse peacekeeper who has to stop smoothing things over. Burned out on crew ego elsewhere; waited a year for Sabine and Reiko to warm up to him; welcomes the PC on night one because he hates friction (see Structural Themes; Open Question 12)
- Buyer, Reiko (a nod to *Tokyo Drift*): sharp and businesslike, cool toward the PC at first ("Wholesale is for crew. You're not crew."); warms up and gives the PC wholesale after Round 1; parts at wholesale until the rift, then full price (canon 15). Works out of Sabine's garage; not Sabine's employee, she just hangs out with her (canon 24). **Arc (first pass 2026-11-05, needs more passes):** protective gatekeeper who gets burned after vouching for the PC. Saw crew politics firsthand back in the day (ties to #3 recognizing her name in Round 1) and left the cliques on purpose (see Structural Themes; Open Question 12)

## 3. The Debt

- Sabine explains the rebuild debt: she fronted the engine, labor, and tow, so the PC races to pay her back until they're even (amount `<TBD: Barry>`)
- Repayment: a share of race winnings goes toward the tab (share size `<TBD: Barry>`) — a personal repair bill, not a crew sponsorship (she stepped away from the scene, canon 14, 24)
- Debt is settled at the end of Round 1, when the PC beats the Street crew boss (canon decision 19, 2026-11-13); after that the PC stays for the fun and the mystery
- PC formally enters the underground scene

# STRUCTURAL THEMES

## Mentor Arc
- Entry point into the underground world
- Walked away from the old generation without a word; still on the scene but keeps clear of every crew; privately accused by the old generation, never publicly
- Took PC in on the surface because she felt responsible for the blown motor and fronted the rebuild; really because she sees herself in PC (never admits it)
- Believes the snitch accusation was the reason; learns the real reasons only at the summit
- Accused a second time, by PC, at the end of Round 3; blows up again when PC digs into her past (“I know what really happened”); the rift stands until the final race at the summit
- Recontextualized rather than simply vindicated

## Player Arc
- outsider → entrant → investigator → accuser → estranged → owns the mistake → integrated member
- Mirror of Sabine: PC walked out on their hometown crew without explaining, and they believe PC ditched them; Sabine was pushed out of hers over a lie
- Hook (Round 1): hearing Sabine’s story of walking away without explanation, PC recognizes their own and starts digging
- PC repeats the Myth Drivers’ mistake: accuses Sabine on bad data. The guilt mirror becomes literal — PC is now the one who wrongly cut someone off
- After the rift: PC knows they messed up and races the Myth Drivers for the truth; then PC convinces them to come apologize too, and owns the mistake around the final race with Sabine
- #3 mirrors PC: both left without facing the goodbye (he let Sabine be pushed out rather than tell her he was leaving)
- Helping Sabine reconcile is PC working through their own guilt by proxy; at the summit Sabine turns it back on them (“And when are you calling them?”), and in the epilogue PC calls the hometown crew and races them for fun, the arc's resolution

## Garage Crew Arcs (Jesse & Reiko — first pass 2026-11-05, needs more passes)

### "Sabine's crew"
- Jesse, Reiko and Malo (the barkeep) are part of an informal off-grid circle of builders and tuners who reject the racing cliques and exist solely for the cars (canon 24)
- That's how Sabine stays isolated from the crews after the sweep yet is still known across the city
- End of Round 1 (around the speakeasy, once the debt clears; moved 2026-11-13): Jesse and Reiko introduce the PC to this circle

### Reiko — protective gatekeeper who gets burned after vouching
- Saw crew politics firsthand back in the day (ties to #3 recognizing her name in Round 1) and left the cliques on purpose
- Prologue → Round 1: cool at first ("Wholesale is for crew"), opens full-price parts after the race 3 win, warms up and gives wholesale at the end of Round 1
- End of Round 1 (the speakeasy): with Jesse, brings the PC into "Sabine's crew"
- End of Round 2 (before the Touge round, 2026-11-13): warns the PC off digging into the sweep. When the PC accuses Sabine anyway, it stings her personally because she let the PC in
- Finale rift (locked, Austin 2026-11-11): drops wholesale, back to full price, but still sells to the PC, including the big Round 3 mods, because she's a business owner. The warm-up (closed, then wholesale, then "Sabine's crew") is what she takes back; business keeps the shop open without breaking the rift
- Once she sees the PC rounding up the Myth Drivers to fix what they broke, she's the one who points the PC to where Sabine runs the mountain alone (first pass for Open Question 8)

### Jesse — conflict-averse peacekeeper who has to stop smoothing things over
- Burned out on crew ego elsewhere; waited a year for Sabine and Reiko to warm up to him; welcomes the PC on night one because he hates friction
- Prologue → Round 3: hooks up the DAQ log after opening race 2 and offers to run back the last race in the garage (canon 29)
- Rounds 1–2: makes the garage and the speakeasy circle feel like a real home, though his no-questions-asked warmth also makes it easy for the PC to keep dodging their past
- End of Round 3 (Mentor Confrontation, after "Get out of my garage."): subverts the Prologue beat where he softened Reiko's edge. Instead of covering for the PC, he refuses to smooth it over and quietly points out that the PC decided without asking. Then he shuts the PC out (locked, Austin 2026-11-11): no tuning or respecs for the PC in the Finale, so the PC goes to the reverse speakeasy and Malo for those, and pulls the DAQ log themselves
- Summit / Epilogue: earning his easy warmth back by the epilogue is what makes the garage feel whole again
