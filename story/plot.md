## 1. Opening Sequence

- PC (mid-20s, any gender) moves to the city for work — really running from something: walked out on their hometown crew without explaining why: the PC was its driver and left the week of a big race for a job in the city, so the crew thinks the PC ditched them for something better; really, the PC felt like the weak link holding them back
- PC personality (locked 2026-11-04, kept light so players can project): deflects about themselves (dodges "where you from?"); quick to decide what other people think (assumed the crew saw them as the weak link, assumes Sabine is the snitch: the same mistake the Myth Drivers made); earnest and driven once locked on
- Arrives at dusk; night from then on
- Nighttime exploration; first exposure to underground racing culture
- Overworld tutorial on the drive home from work at dusk
- On a back road, a car flashes its headlights: first race challenge (hazards to accept)
- First race (battle tutorial); PC ignores a manifold warning and the engine blows
- The challenger (Sabine) comes back; PC deflects her questions about where they’re from; she tows the car to her shop
- Why she takes PC in: on the surface, her garage hasn’t had a real driver since the breakup and crews won’t take her seriously without one; underneath, she sees herself in PC — someone who showed up alone and won’t say why (she never admits it)

Works out of Sabine's garage; not Sabine's employee, he just hangs out with her (canon 24). **Arc (first pass 2026-11-05, needs more passes):** conflict-averse peacekeeper who has to stop smoothing things over. # STRUCTURAL THEMES

## Mentor Arc
- Entry point into the underground world
- Walked away from the old generation without a word; still on the scene but keeps clear of every crew; privately accused by the old generation, never publicly
- Took PC in for a driver; really because she sees herself in PC (never admits it)
- Believes the snitch accusation was the reason; learns the real reasons only at the summit
- Accused a second time, by PC, at the end of Round 3; blows up again when PC digs into her past (“I know what really happened”); the rift stands until the final race at the summit
- Recontextualized rather than simply vindicated

## Player Arc
- outsider → entrant → investigator → accuser → estranged → owns the mistake → integrated member
- Mirror of Sabine: PC walked out on their hometown crew without explaining, and they believe PC ditched them; Sabine was pushed out of hers over a lie
- Hook (Round 1): hearing Sabine’s story of walking away without explanation, PC recognizes their own and starts digging
- PC repeats the Myth Drivers’ mistake: accuses Sabine on bad data. The guilt mirror becomes literal — PC is now the one who wrongly cut someone off
- After the rift: PC knows they messed up and races the Myth Drivers for the truth; then PC convinces them to come apologize too, and owns the mistake around the final race with Sabine
- #3 mirrors PC: both left without facing the goodbye (he let Sabine be pushed out rather than tell her he was leaving)
- Helping Sabine reconcile is PC working through their own guilt by proxy; at the summit Sabine turns it back on them (“And when are you calling them?”), and in the epilogue PC calls the hometown crew and races them for fun, the arc's resolution
