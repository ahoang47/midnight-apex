# Corner Lines: In-World Reasons

Narrative flavor for the district corner lines. This file has no numbers. The letters (Defend, Steady, Attack for Entry/Apex/Exit) are Barry's `LINE_SET_C` in `sim/velocity_shift/equations.py`, read at `6fc87d5`. Adherence and alt weights (`LINE_ADHERENCE`, `LINE_ALT_WEIGHTS`) are Barry's too. If the table changes, the letters here follow it and only the reasons get rewritten.

Alternative lines keep the main Entry. alt1 changes Apex and Exit, alt2 changes only Exit. The lane bluff (sitting in the wrong lane, design 06) is a separate layer and isn't covered here.

## Main lines

| Corner | Touge | Wangan | Street |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hairpin | DSA: brake inside, roll the apex, drive off the turn to carry momentum down the hill | DSA: the same textbook hairpin, since highway crews treat a hairpin as a tollbooth, not a fight | DDA: hold the inside through the apex so nobody gets a nose in, then go |
| Constant radius | SSA: hold the arc, carry speed, power out | SAA: the road stays open, so commit from the apex on | DSD: cover the inside, ride it out, cover again on exit, position over speed |
| Decreasing radius | SDS: the mountain pinches, so protect at the late apex and smooth out | DDS: professionals don't gamble on a closing corner | DDS: door shut until it opens |
| Increasing radius | SSA: patient, then let the road open up | AAD: the road opens, so go flat out, then shut the door on the exit so the car behind can't use your draft on the next straight | SSA: even Street lets an opening corner run |
| 90-degree | DSA: square it off, drive out | DSA: same | SSA: it's an intersection, so you can't dive the corner of the block. Hold your lane through the cross street, then punch it out |
| Chicane | SDA: settle, protect the middle flick, drive out | DSA: brake early, keep it tidy, go | DAA: protect the entry, then muscle through the flick |
| Esses | SSS: flow, with no stance changes and no lost momentum | SSS: same, since nobody wants to be the one who breaks rhythm on a bend | ASA: throw it in, settle, throw it out, loud at both ends |
| Sweeper | SSA: hold the arc, power at the end | AAD: flat out through the long bend, then close the door on the exit before the straight | DDD: a long corner is a long time to defend |

District habits, for barks: Touge preserves momentum (mostly Steady, Attack only on exits). Wangan commits where the road opens and shuts the door before the straights. Street fights for position (Defend-heavy, Attack as a lunge), except at intersections, where it waits out the cross street.

## Alternative lines

An alternative is the line a driver takes when they think you've learned their district's habit. The lane and Entry stay honest; the difference shows at the apex (alt1) or only at the exit (alt2). By rung (pending Open Question 17):
- Kids: alts read as nerve or ego, not craft, either a cocky overcommit or a flinch.
- Mature members: deliberate counters ("I know you know").
- Old heads: habits from "that night" and the old generation, invisible until the apex and the hardest to learn.
- Crew boss: uses both, framed by personality (big brother, promoter, influencer).

### Street (influencer boss, a loud, online, clout-driven crew, old heads who paid for the sweep)
Street is the thinnest district, so its alternatives should be the most legible and personality-driven. A player who has listened to the Street crew should be able to guess who deviates and why.
| Corner | Main | alt1: who and why | alt2: who and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hairpin | DDA | DAS: Punk-type kid's dive-bomb for the clip lunges at the apex and can't put the power down | DDS: old head who did sixty days doesn't floor it out of anything anymore |
| Constant radius | DSD | DDA: mature member blocks the whole arc, then gets greedy at the exit | DSA: kid forgets the plan and floors it on exit ("for the camera") |
| Decreasing radius | DDS | DAD: influencer lunges at the pinch for the shot, then shuts the door | DDD: Mechanic-type mature member protects the car and the position |
| Increasing radius | SSA | SDS: mature member, the group-chat theorist. "Everyone says you counter the opener," so he covers mid-corner | SSS: old head lets you go and saves it for the next block |
| 90-degree | SSA | SDS: old head. Corners of the block are for blocking, so he sits on the apex and rolls out calm | SSS: kid who got pulled over filming this intersection last month stays off the throttle out of the 90 |
| Chicane | DAA | DSS: influencer smooths the flick so the onboard footage stays clean | DAS: old head muscles the middle but stays calm on exit |
| Esses | ASA | ADS: mature member throws it in, then shuts the second half | ASS: kid throws it in loud, then runs out of nerve |
| Sweeper | DDD | DAA: kid gets bored blocking and floors it, the cocky overcommit | DDA: influencer blocks all the way, then sends it for the finish-line shot |

Street story hook: kids announce their habits on stream ("Watch me send it into turn two"), which makes Street's alts the easiest to learn from barks. The sixty-days old head's hairpin alt2 (never floors exits) is the mechanical echo of his reveal line.
