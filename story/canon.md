## Decisions

1. **Three Myth Drivers, one per district.** #1 Touge, #2 Wangan, #3 Street. There is no #4.
2. **No informant.** The police sweep was benign and coincidental. The snitch accusation was a convenient story each Myth Driver used, privately, to cover his own personal reason for pushing Sabine out:
   - #1 — distrust of her uncle-dad, a detective (all three Myth Drivers knew about her uncle; it was no secret, but only #1 held it against her)
   - #2 — she turned down his date request
   - #3 — he wanted out to go pro and didn't want her hurt by him leaving the group, so he was glad she was gone; letting her be pushed out was easier on his feelings and harder on hers (see 9)
   - Always called the **police sweep** (not "sting operation").
   - **The accusation never spread.** Only the three Myth Drivers and Sabine know about it, and that she was pushed out. They froze her out quietly. The wider scene only knows she vanished without a word around the time of the sweep; crews speculate and misremember, but nobody names a snitch accusation.
3. **Sabine doesn't know.** She genuinely believes she was frozen out over the snitch accusation and learns the real reasons only at the end. She has never told anyone about it.
4. **Climax at the summit.** There is no trick. The player honestly invites Sabine to the summit of **Fushigi-Yama**, the Touge district's mountain, to apologize ("I was wrong. Be there." — first-pass line), the first time the PC breaks their pattern of leaving without explanation. The three Myth Drivers each, separately, catch wind that the PC is apologizing to Sabine, feel guilty, and show up on their own, perhaps also wanting to apologize `<TODO: how word reaches the Myth Drivers>`. They don't coordinate; the summit is still their first meeting since the breakup, and it's where the truth comes out. Racing each Myth Driver in his district during the Finale stays.
5. **Ending.** Sabine calmly declines their offer to rejoin, for two real reasons: she has the player's crew now (which requires the PC's apology and repair after the rift — see 15), and #3 let her be pushed out to spare himself a goodbye (see 9), then gets in one cutting parting shot: `<TODO: parting shot — Austin to approve>`.
6. **Setting.** A fictional mashup city, neither Japan nor the US. No real-world places or currencies. City name, in-world district names and currency are `<TBD>`.
7. **Time of day.** The opening happens at dusk; the game is at night from then on.
8. **Gender.** Sabine is always female. The player character can be any gender.
9. **Myth Driver #3 is the Street specialist.** Circuit and street racing are the same idea in this universe. He wanted out of the group to go pro in the circuit. He was glad Sabine was gone, not because he didn't want her around, but because he didn't want her hurt by him leaving. Letting her be pushed out was easier on his feelings and harder on hers. He never actually went pro: he let her be pushed out for a career that never happened `<TBD: why his pro career fell through>`. This is also a real reason Sabine won't rejoin him and the old generation at the end. He mirrors the PC: leaving without facing the goodbye.
10. **Silence since the breakup.** The Myth Drivers haven't spoken since the breakup. Anything about them talking to each other happened before it. The summit is their first meeting since.
11. **Act structure.** Prologue → Rounds 1–3 → Finale.
    - **Prologue:** dusk arrival, the overworld tutorial on the drive home from work, the race with Sabine, the blowout, the garage intro and the debt.
    - **Rounds 1–3:** each district has exactly one crew and one crew boss (three crews, three crew bosses in total). Each round the player faces each district’s crew and its crew boss in Touge, then Wangan, then Street, in that order; Rounds 2 and 3 are rematches against the same crew, which gets stronger every time. Every crew defeat reveals another piece of the police sweep mystery.
    - **Round themes:** Round 1 = the incident, Round 2 = its consequences across the scene, Round 3 = distortion (what people misremember), each seen from every district.
    - **Finale:** finishing Round 3 unlocks the three Myth Driver encounters (one per district), then the Fushigi-Yama summit and the ending.
    - The main plot stays grounded; JRPG weirdness and extra lore go in substories and side quests.
12. **Street cred gates the rotation.** After the player beats a crew, it won't give them a rematch until they've proven themselves in the other districts: they don't have enough street cred yet. Cred is earned by beating the other districts' crews, which is why each round rotates Touge → Wangan → Street. Street cred is an in-world lore explanation only, not a tracked stat.
13. **Player character (first pass).** The PC is mid-20s and any gender, moved to the city for work, but is really running from something: they walked out on their own crew/family without explaining why, and the people they left believe the PC betrayed them. This mirrors Sabine, who was pushed out of her crew over a lie.
    - **The PC's hook:** in Round 1 the PC hears Sabine's story (someone who vanished without explanation, whom people assume had something to hide) and recognizes their own. Nobody calls Sabine a snitch there; the parallel is implied.
    - Helping Sabine reconcile is the PC working through their own guilt by proxy. At the summit Sabine may turn it back on them ("And when are you calling them?" — `<PROPOSAL>`), likely landing after the PC's apology. After the rift (15) the mirror is literal: the PC accused Sabine on bad data, just as the Myth Drivers did.
14. **Why Sabine takes the PC in.** Practical on the surface: her garage hasn't had a real driver since the breakup, and crews won't take her seriously without one. Personal underneath: she sees herself in the PC, someone who showed up alone and won't say why. She never admits the second.
15. **The PC puts it together — wrongly.** Nobody tells the PC about the snitch accusation. They work it out themselves from clues scattered across the crew reveals in Rounds 1–3, and the clues are bad data: they genuinely point toward Sabine's guilt. At the Mentor Confrontation (locked: end of Round 3) the PC accuses Sabine of being the snitch. She is hurt and refuses to defend herself, or denies it `<TODO: Sabine's reaction>`. It blows up their relationship, and the rift stands going into the Finale. Only at the summit does it come out that the police sweep was benign — the PC made the same mistake the Myth Drivers did.
16. **The PC's motive after the rift.** Two things drive the PC to chase the Myth Drivers and set up the summit: doubt (the Myth Drivers' accounts contradict each other, and the PC starts to suspect they were wrong about Sabine) and guilt (the rift echoes what the PC did back home, and they won't let it end the same way). Doubt drives the racing; guilt makes it personal.
17. **Myth Driver #2's guilt leaks.** In his Finale race, #2 hints that Sabine may have been a scapegoat, because he feels guilty about ousting her. It's one of the first cracks in the PC's certainty (feeds decision 16's doubt) and foreshadows him coming to the summit to apologize.
18. **Myth Driver #1's contradiction (first pass).** In his Finale race, #1 insists Sabine left on her own the night of the sweep. Against #2's hints that she was pushed, it's the first time two Myth Driver accounts can't both be true, and it starts the PC's doubt (16).

## Racer Types and Archetypes

"Racer type" is the story role (Delinquent, Salaryman, ...). "Archetype" is the driving style from the sim (`sim/velocity_shift/`). Each district has exactly one racer type per archetype; each racer type always uses its archetype. Only named rivals may break the pattern, on purpose.

| Archetype | Touge | Wangan | Street |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aggressive**: attacks right away | Delinquent | Executive | Punk |
| **Defensive**: waits, then punishes your habits | Gang Leader | Office Lady | Mechanic |
| **Precise**: always makes the smartest move | Drifter | Doctor | Engineer |
| **Balanced**: follows a set rhythm | High Schooler | Salaryman | Skater |
| **Adaptive**: cautious early, sharper later | Honor Student | Consultant | Influencer |

Never reuse archetype names as character or racer-type names. Crew boss stakes are money, rep, rare shop mods and story progress; losing a crew boss race means a retry, never losing the car.

## Backstory Timeline

1. **The old generation.** Sabine races alongside the three Myth Drivers. Pressure builds privately: everyone in the group knows her uncle-dad is a detective, and #1 never trusts her because of it; #2 resents the turned-down date (it stayed private; he wrongly believes she told people) `<CHECK: rewritten for private-rumor canon — Austin to confirm>`, #3 wants out to go pro and dreads telling her he's leaving.
2. **The police sweep.** A benign, coincidental sweep hits the scene. Privately, the three Myth Drivers accuse Sabine of being the snitch, each using it as cover for his own reason, and freeze her out quietly. The accusation never leaves the four of them.
3. **The breakup.** The old generation fractures. To the wider scene, Sabine simply vanished without a word around the time of the sweep. The Myth Drivers stop speaking to each other and fade into semi-retired legend. #3's pro career never happens `<TBD: why his pro career fell through>`. Sabine builds her own garage crew, still believing the accusation was the reason.
4. **The player arrives.** The player moves to the city at dusk. A first race with Sabine ends in a blown engine; she tows the car to her garage and the player enters the scene owing a repair debt. She needs a real driver; she also sees herself in the PC (unspoken).
5. **Rounds 1–3.** Each round the player beats the same three crews and their crew bosses (each stronger than last time) in Touge, then Wangan, then Street, earning the street cred for the next rematch; every crew defeat reveals another piece of the mystery of her disappearance, and in Round 1 the PC recognizes their own story in hers. Sabine deflects. The PC pieces together the accusation from misleading clues in the crew reveals and, at the end of Round 3, accuses Sabine of being the snitch. The relationship blows up `<TODO: Sabine's reaction>`.
6. **The Myth Drivers.** Finishing Round 3 unlocks a race with each Myth Driver: Touge (#1), Wangan (#2), Street (#3). The player races them while estranged from Sabine and gathers contradictory accounts.
7. **The summit.** The player honestly asks Sabine to meet at the summit of Fushigi-Yama to apologize ("I was wrong. Be there."). The three Myth Drivers each hear about it separately, feel guilty, and come on their own. The truth comes out: the sweep was benign. Sabine learns the real reasons for the first time, and the PC realizes they were wrong too and has to own it.
8. **Ending.** They reconcile and offer Sabine her place back. After the PC's apology, she calmly declines (she has the player's crew, and she won't go back to the group #3 let her be pushed out of), and gets in one parting shot.
