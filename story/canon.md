## Decisions

1. **Three Myth Drivers, one per district.** #1 Touge, #2 Wangan, #3 Street. There is no #4.
2. **No informant.** The police sweep was benign and coincidental. The snitch accusation was a convenient story each Myth Driver used, privately, to cover his own personal reason for pushing Sabine out:
   - #1 — distrust of her uncle-dad, a detective (all three Myth Drivers knew about her uncle; it was no secret, but only #1 held it against her)
   - #2 — she turned down his date request
   - #3 — he wanted out to go pro and didn't want her hurt by him leaving the group, so he was glad she was gone; letting her be pushed out was easier on his feelings and harder on hers (see 9)
   - Always called the **police sweep** (not "sting operation").
   - **The accusation never spread.** Only the three Myth Drivers and Sabine know about it, and that she was pushed out. They froze her out quietly. The wider scene only knows she walked away from the old generation without a word around the time of the sweep and hasn't run with any crew since. She's still around (she runs her own garage and still drives), she just doesn't mess with the crews; crews speculate and misremember, but nobody names a snitch accusation.
3. **Sabine doesn't know.** She genuinely believes she was frozen out over the snitch accusation and learns the real reasons only at the end. She has never told anyone about it.
4. **Climax at the summit (restructured 2026-11-02).** There is no trick. After the Finale races the PC knows what really happened (16) and goes to Sabine: "I know what really happened." She blows up at the PC for digging into her past, a second wound after the accusation (15), and the PC leaves. The PC then convinces all three Myth Drivers to come and apologize to her too: one big apology party. The climax is a final race between the PC and Sabine at the summit of **Fushigi-Yama**, the Touge district's mountain `<TODO: how the PC gets Sabine to the final race>`. The PC's apology and the Myth Drivers' apologies happen around the race `<TODO: before, during or after the race>`. The summit is still the Myth Drivers' first meeting since the breakup, and it's where Sabine learns their real reasons. Racing each Myth Driver in his district during the Finale stays.
5. **Ending.** After the final race and the apology party, Sabine calmly declines their offer to rejoin, for three real reasons (the third: nobody ever acknowledged the family emergency she left for the night of the sweep, decision 23), the first two being: she has the player's crew now (which requires the PC's apology and repair after the rift — see 15), and #3 let her be pushed out to spare himself a goodbye (see 9), then gets in one cutting parting shot: "My uncle's doing fine, by the way. Since none of you ever asked."
6. **Setting.** A fictional mashup city, neither Japan nor the US. No real-world places or currencies. City name, in-world district names and currency are `<TBD>`.
7. **Time of day.** The opening happens at dusk; the game is at night from then on.
8. **Gender.** Sabine is always female. The player character can be any gender.
9. **Myth Driver #3 is the Street specialist.** Circuit and street racing are the same idea in this universe. He wanted out of the group to go pro in the circuit. He was glad Sabine was gone, not because he didn't want her around, but because he didn't want her hurt by him leaving. Letting her be pushed out was easier on his feelings and harder on hers. He never actually went pro: he let her be pushed out for a career that never happened `<TBD: why his pro career fell through>`. This is also a real reason Sabine won't rejoin him and the old generation at the end. He mirrors the PC: leaving without facing the goodbye.
10. **Silence since the breakup.** The Myth Drivers haven't spoken since the breakup. Anything about them talking to each other happened before it. The summit is their first meeting since.
11. **Act structure.** Prologue → Rounds 1–3 → Finale.
    - **Prologue:** dusk arrival, the overworld tutorial on the drive home from work, the race with Sabine, the blowout, the garage intro and the debt.
    - **Rounds 1–3:** each district has exactly one crew and one crew boss (three crews, three crew bosses in total). Each round the player faces each district’s crew and its crew boss in Touge, then Wangan, then Street, in that order; Rounds 2 and 3 are rematches against the same crew, which gets stronger every time. Every round, the player runs through the whole crew in rank order: first its kids who inherited the myth (they believe the legend as told), then its more mature members who know not everything is what it seems, then its old heads who were there, and finally the crew boss. When the player comes back the next round, the boss has leveled up the whole crew. Flavor text after each race reveals a little more. That progression matches the contradicting clues the PC uncovers across the game. Every crew defeat reveals another piece of the police sweep mystery.
    - **Round themes:** Round 1 = the incident, Round 2 = its consequences across the scene, Round 3 = distortion (what people misremember), each seen from every district.
    - **Myth Driver sightings:** each Myth Driver makes a first sighting right after his district's Round 1 crew is cleared: #1 after Round 1 Touge, #2 after Round 1 Wangan, #3 after Round 1 Street. At each sighting the Myth Driver talks with the PC; there's no race yet. The conversations build the mythos around the fallout and drive the PC to dig further, but never reveal the private accusation. The races stay in the Finale, in the same order: Touge (#1) → Wangan (#2) → Street (#3).
    - **Finale:** finishing Round 3 unlocks the three Myth Driver encounters (one per district), then the Fushigi-Yama summit and the ending.
    - The main plot stays grounded; JRPG weirdness and extra lore go in substories and side quests.
12. **Street cred gates the rotation.** After the player beats a crew, it won't give them a rematch until they've proven themselves in the other districts: they don't have enough street cred yet. Cred is earned by beating the other districts' crews, which is why each round rotates Touge → Wangan → Street. Street cred is an in-world lore explanation only, not a tracked stat.
13. **Player character (first pass).** The PC is mid-20s and any gender, moved to the city for work, but is really running from something: they walked out on their hometown crew without explaining why. The PC was the crew's driver and left the week of a big race for a job in the city without telling anyone, so the crew believes the PC ditched them for something better. The real reason (locked 2026-11-02): the PC felt like the weak link, believed they were holding the crew back and that it would do better without them, and left rather than say so. The crew never saw it that way. It's a misread, like the Myth Drivers', and the epilogue race shows the crew wanted the PC all along. This mirrors Sabine, who was pushed out of her crew over a lie.
    - **The PC's hook:** in Round 1 the PC hears Sabine's story (someone who walked away from her crew without explanation, whom some people assume had something to hide) and recognizes their own. Nobody calls Sabine a snitch there; the parallel is implied.
    - Helping Sabine reconcile is the PC working through their own guilt by proxy. After her parting shot at the summit, Sabine turns it back on them: "And when are you calling them?" After the rift (15) the mirror is literal: the PC accused Sabine on bad data, just as the Myth Drivers did.
    - **Epilogue (locked 2026-11-02):** the PC makes the call themselves, sending the hometown crew "I was wrong. Be there." Nobody else brings the crew (no clips or luck), or the PC's arc wouldn't resolve. The crew comes to Fushigi-Yama. After a short, awkward apology the crew doesn't fully forgive yet, they break the tension with a race ("Talk's cheap. Race us." — first pass). It's a low-stakes, just-for-fun downhill run that doesn't change the ending whether the PC wins or loses, possibly with Sabine, Jesse and Reiko watching. The race tuning belongs to Barry.
14. **Why Sabine takes the PC in.** Practical on the surface: her garage hasn't had a real driver since the breakup, and crews won't take her seriously without one. Personal underneath: she sees herself in the PC, someone who showed up alone and won't say why. She never admits the second.
15. **The PC puts it together — wrongly.** Nobody tells the PC about the snitch accusation. They work it out themselves from clues scattered across the crew reveals in Rounds 1–3, and the clues are bad data: they genuinely point toward Sabine's guilt. At the Mentor Confrontation (locked: end of Round 3) the PC accuses Sabine of being the snitch. She doesn't defend herself (locked 2026-11-02). She's been accused of this once already, and she won't waste the energy again. It's tired, not proud. After a long silence: "Get out of my garage." Jesse and Reiko stay quiet in the background `<PROPOSAL>`. It blows up their relationship, and the rift stands going into the Finale. The PC knows they messed up, and the Finale races are how they learn the police sweep was benign (16): the PC made the same mistake the Myth Drivers did.
16. **The PC's motive after the rift (restructured 2026-11-02).** The PC knows they messed up and wants the truth, so they race all three Myth Drivers (Touge #1 → Wangan #2 → Street #3) to settle the story once and for all. 20. **Not everyone holds it against her.** Most of the scene isn't bitter toward Sabine; they respect her and are puzzled by why she walked away. Suspicion comes from a few voices (gossiping kids, people repeating rumors), not from everyone.

21. **The Wangan crew boss is a promoter.** He organizes the highway runs, takes bets and gets a cut, and sees the PC as a headliner he can sell. He's pragmatic about Sabine, not bitter: her walking away cost the scene its best draw. After Round 2 he opens a high-stakes betting side game for extra money (mechanics are Barry's `<TBD>`). **Old flame (private):** he and Sabine had a thing back in the old generation's day, which is the real reason she turned down Myth Driver #2's date. He reflects a lot of modern car culture (clout, builds made for the camera), in sharp contrast to the old generation. First pass: he idolized #3 growing up and felt abandoned when #3 left to "go pro".

It stings more because the man she was caring for is the "cop uncle" #1 distrusted. That's a third reason she won't rejoin at the end (alongside the PC's crew and #3, decision 5).

## Racer Types and Archetypes

"Racer type" is the story role (Delinquent, Salaryman, ...). "Archetype" is the driving style from the sim (`sim/velocity_shift/`). Each district has exactly one racer type per archetype; each racer type always uses its archetype. Only named rivals may break the pattern, on purpose.

| Archetype | Touge | Wangan | Street |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aggressive**: attacks right away | Delinquent | Executive | Punk |
| **Defensive**: waits, then punishes your habits | Gang Leader | Office Lady | Mechanic |
| **Precise**: always makes the smartest move | Drifter | Doctor | Engineer |
| **Balanced**: follows a set rhythm | High Schooler | Salaryman | Skater |
| **Adaptive**: cautious early, sharper later | Honor Student | Consultant | Influencer |

Never reuse archetype names as character or racer-type names. Crew boss stakes are money, rep, rare shop mods and story progress; losing a crew boss race means a retry, never losing the car.

## Backstory Timeline

1. **The old generation.** Sabine races alongside the three Myth Drivers. Pressure builds privately: everyone in the group knows her uncle-dad is a detective, and #1 never trusts her because of it; #2 resents the turned-down date. Sabine never told anyone; #2 vented about it to #1 and #3 himself one night, they teased him for weeks, and he convinced himself the jokes started with her (locked 2026-11-02), #3 wants out to go pro and dreads telling her he's leaving.
2. **The police sweep.** A large, coordinated sweep hits all three districts (Touge, Wangan, Street) in one night: lots of tickets and impounds, maybe a few arrests, but routine enforcement, not a tip-off. Minutes before it starts, Sabine gets a call from her uncle-dad about an unrelated family emergency (he's had a heart attack) and leaves; she's the only one of the old generation who walks away clean. The scene goes quiet for months afterward (the "collapse"). Privately, the three Myth Drivers accuse Sabine of being the snitch, each using it as cover for his own reason, and freeze her out quietly. The accusation never leaves the four of them.
3. **The breakup.** The old generation fractures. To the wider scene, Sabine simply walked away from the old generation without a word around the time of the sweep. She stays on the scene but keeps clear of every crew. The Myth Drivers stop speaking to each other and fade into semi-retired legend. #3's pro career never happens `<TBD: why his pro career fell through>`. Sabine builds her own garage crew, still believing the accusation was the reason.
4. **The player arrives.** The player moves to the city at dusk. A first race with Sabine ends in a blown engine; she tows the car to her garage and the player enters the scene owing a repair debt. She needs a real driver; she also sees herself in the PC (unspoken).
5. **Rounds 1–3.** Each round the player beats the same three crews and their crew bosses (running through each whole crew from its kids to its mature members to its old heads to the boss, and each crew leveled up since last time) in Touge, then Wangan, then Street, earning the street cred for the next rematch; every crew defeat reveals another piece of the mystery of her disappearance, and in Round 1 the PC recognizes their own story in hers. In Round 1 each Myth Driver also makes a first sighting after his district's crew is cleared (no race yet). Sabine deflects. The PC pieces together the accusation from misleading clues in the crew reveals and, at the end of Round 3, accuses Sabine of being the snitch. She doesn't defend herself ("Get out of my garage."), and the relationship blows up.
6. **The Myth Drivers.** Finishing Round 3 unlocks a race with each Myth Driver: Touge (#1), Wangan (#2), Street (#3). Knowing they messed up, the player races them while estranged from Sabine to find the truth. Their accounts contradict each other, the snitch story unravels, and the PC learns the sweep was benign and each Myth Driver's real reason.
7. **The blowup.** The PC goes to Sabine: "I know what really happened." She blows up at the PC for digging into her past, and the PC leaves. The PC convinces all three Myth Drivers to come and apologize too: one big apology party.
8. **The summit.** The PC and Sabine race at the summit of Fushigi-Yama `<TODO: how the PC gets Sabine to the final race>`. The PC and the three Myth Drivers apologize around the race `<TODO: before, during or after the race>`. Sabine learns the real reasons for the first time, from them.
9. **Ending.** They reconcile and offer Sabine her place back. After the PC's apology, she calmly declines (she has the player's crew, and she won't go back to the group #3 let her be pushed out of), and gets in one parting shot. Then she asks the PC, "And when are you calling them?"
10. **Epilogue.** The PC calls their hometown crew ("I was wrong. Be there."). The crew comes to Fushigi-Yama, the PC apologizes, and they run a low-stakes race for fun. The PC ends the game part of the scene with both crews.
