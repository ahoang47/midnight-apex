# 1. Wild Race (Opponent Initiates)

These should be short and highly repeatable.

**Purpose:** Make the city feel alive, deliver quick personality snapshots, trigger combat efficiently.

**Recommended Total Dialogue:** 1–2 pre-race lines, 1–3 mid-race lines, 1 win/loss line.

**Must-have Wild Race Characters (2026-11-06):** Tourist groups like the ones that go to Daikoku PA in real life (in-world equivalents only; district/archetype mapping `<TBD>`).

## Pre-Battle Dialogue

Opponent notices player.

- "You new around here? Let's party." 
- "I hear you're pretty fast. Let's put it to the test."
- "You really showed up with that build? I was hoping for a challenge."
- "Try to keep up. I don't want this ending before it even starts."
- "I've already memorized every corner of this route. You're just background noise."
- "I didn't come here to race you. I came here to set a time you'll never touch."
- "I'll give you one warning: stay in my rearview, where you belong."
- "If you blink, it's over. If you hesitate, you lose."
- "Let's make this quick. I've got better opponents waiting further up the food chain."
- "You're not racing me. You're chasing a shadow you can't catch."
- "I hope you brought insurance. You're about to need it."

## During Battle Dialogue

### Even State — DP Difference Within Delta

**Purpose:** Maintain tension, indicate parity.

#### Aggressive
- "Stay in my lane and you might finish this race intact."
- "You're not racing me, you're surviving me."
- "I don't brake. I break people."
- "Keep pushing. I want to see what snaps first: your nerve or your car."
- "You're in my way. That's your first mistake."

#### Territorial
- "This stretch of road is mine. You're just passing through."
- "You crossed into my world the moment you lined up."
- "I've owned every racer on this strip. You won't be the exception."
- "Give up now or learn why nobody comes back here twice."

#### Opportunistic
- "I don't need to be faster. I just need you to slip once."
- "All I hear is tire noise and bad decisions."
- "Keep leading. It makes the fall easier to time."
- "You're doing great… right up until you aren't."

#### Cocky
- "You're actually keeping up? Cute."
- "That's all you got? I could do this in reverse."
- "Don't blink — you might miss your chance to lose properly."
- "I'm sandbagging to keep it fun for you."

#### Curious
- "You're different… let's see how long that lasts."
- "Interesting line choice. Who taught you that?"
- "You don't drive like the others. That's either talent or ignorance."
- "How far are you willing to push this before you fold?"
- "Show me what kind of problem you are."

### Player Advantage — Player DP Exceeds Delta

**Purpose:** Show opponent stress.

#### Denial (refusing to accept gap)
- "No—no way you're that far ahead already!"
- "That's not possible… I had the better launch!"
- "No way, you probably have more than 100,000 <currency> under the hood!"
- "This is a trick. It has to be a trick!"
- "I refuse to believe I'm losing this badly!"

#### Mechanical Panic (car + driver strain)
- "My turbo's screaming—this thing can't keep up!"
- "Temperature's spiking—what are you even DOING up there?!"
- "My manifold's gonna blow if we keep this up."
- "This engine wasn't built for this kind of monster gap!"
- "Something's breaking—something is DEFINITELY breaking!"

#### Frustrated Desperation (forcing risk)
- "I don't care—I'm catching you even if I blow the block!"
- "Just one mistake! That's all I need from you!"
- "Why won't you slow down?! You're supposed to slow down!"
- "Stop accelerating like that's normal!"
- "I can still bring this back—I have to!"

#### Existential Shock (skill gap realization)
- "That line… that speed… you're not racing like a human…"
- "What kind of monster takes that corner flat-out?!"
- "This isn't my tier… I'm not even in your league!"
- "You're rewriting what 'fast' even means!"

#### Breakdown / Anime Collapse Energy
- "Wait—WAIT—WHY ARE YOU STILL GAINING DISTANCE?!"
- "NO NO NO NO NO—this can't be happening!!"
- "I'm full throttle!! I'M FULL THROTTLE!!"
- "Someone explain this gap to me RIGHT NOW!"
- "I can't even SEE your taillights anymore…!"

#### Quiet Horror (low voice realization)
- "...you're gone."
- "That gap shouldn't exist."
- "I did everything right… and you still left me."
- "This race ended a while ago, didn't it…"
- "Damn… losing the line. Gotta lock in."

### Opponent Advantage — Opponent DP Exceeds Delta

**Purpose:** Increase pressure.

#### Controlled Dominance (calm, efficient lead)
- "You're still there? I expected you to drop off by now."
- "Keep pace if you can. I'm not adjusting for you."
- "This is the rhythm I set. You're just reacting."
- "View up here is nice. You should try it sometime."
- "Every corner you chase, I've already solved."

#### Pressure Application (forcing mistakes from the front)
- "I don't need to open the gap. You'll do it for me."
- "Try and stay close. It makes it easier to see your mistakes."
- "You're overdriving already—I can hear it."
- "How's the view from back there?"

#### Territorial Assertion (ownership of space)
- "This is my road, I own it, you're just driving in it."

#### Cold Confidence (minimal emotion, high certainty)
- "You're not closing this gap."
- "Too easy."

#### Taunting Control (playful superiority)
- "You're doing great—for second place."
- "I'll slow down for you later. Maybe."
- "Careful, you're starting to look committed."
- "I can hear your engine working harder than mine."
- "If you're trying to scare me, try harder."

## Finish State

### Opponent Defeat
- "...Fine. You earned it."
- "Don't get comfortable."
- "You drive different."
- "I almost had you."
- "You came looking for this."
- "Guess you knew what you were doing."

### Player Defeat
- "Come back after tuning that heap."
- "That's what experience looks like."
- "Next time, pick easier targets."
- "You never had me. You never had your car."

# 2. Wild Race (Player Initiates)

These should feel slightly more predatory/confident.

**Purpose:** Reinforce player agency, let player feel like the hunter.

**Recommended Total Dialogue:** Similar length to opponent-initiated wild races.

## Pre-Battle Dialogue

Opponent reacts to being challenged.

- "You picking fights now?"
- "That car… is that supposed to scare me? Don't make me laugh."
- "You sure about this?"
- "Didn't think you had the nerve."
- "You got the money to back up that noise?"
- "You're really talking like that before the race even starts?"
- "All that confidence… and you haven't even seen me race yet."
- "You sure you want to say that before I gap you?"
- "Don't run your mouth if your driving can't match it, kid."
- "I don't know if you're confident or just delusional."

# 3. Lieutenant Race (crew members)

These are mini-bosses.

**Purpose:** Represent organized faction identity, deliver memorable personalities, gate district progression.

**Recommended Total Dialogue:** 3–6 pre-race lines, 5–10 mid-race lines, 2–4 finish lines.

Crew members should reference their crew, reference district politics, reference reputation, and taunt strategically.

## Initial Interaction

Crew member acknowledges where the PC is in the story. Reputation is lore only (canon 12), so these tiers are keyed to story progress, not a stat. Round 1–3 here map to Street, then Wangan, then Touge, not rematches with the same crew.

### Round 1
- "You haven't earned the right to challenge us."
- "Beat more street runners first."
- "Your name hasn't reached this crew."
- "Come back when the district talks about you."

### Round 2
- "Heard about you."
- "You're moving up faster than expected."
- "You've been causing problems."

### Round 3
- "So you're the one everyone's talking about."
- "I was wondering when you'd show up."
- "About time."

## Reputation Gate Dialogue Options

These are important because they create roleplay identity.

### Aggressive Response
- "Then I'll earn it by beating you."
- "Reputation's built on the road."
- "Talk less. Race more."

**Crew response:**
- "Bold."
- "Confidence or stupidity?"

### Respectful Response
- "Then point me toward someone worth racing."
- "I'll come back stronger."
- "Fair enough."

**Crew response:**
- "At least you understand hierarchy."
- "Good. Learn the district first."

### Arrogant Response
- "Your crew scared?"
- "Maybe your standards are dropping."
- "Sounds like excuses."

**Crew response:**
- "Careful."
- "You're testing my patience."
- "Keep talking."

### Neutral/Professional Response
- "What's the requirement?"
- "Who do I need to beat?"
- "How much reputation do you want?"

**Crew response:**
- "Direct. I respect that."
- "Beat our runners first."
- "Earn your place."

## During Crew Battles

Crew dialogue should evolve across battle phases.

### Even State
- "Good. You actually understand pressure."
- "Most racers fold before this point."
- "You're keeping pace."

### Player Advantage
- "...You're reading me."
- "This isn't supposed to happen."
- "So the rumors were true."

### Opponent Advantage
- "This is the difference experience makes."
- "Your fundamentals are weak."
- "You haven't mastered your machine yet."

### Critical / Near Finish

Use overheating or desperation language.

- "Your car's reaching its limit."
- "You're burning everything for this?"
- "One mistake and you're done."

# 4. Crew Boss Race (Blacklist equivalent)

These are major narrative events.

**Purpose:** Mark each district’s crew boss as the climax of that district’s round, deliver iconic personalities, reward progression, create emotional stakes.

**Recommended Total Dialogue:** 8–15 pre-race lines, 10–20 mid-race lines, 4–8 finish lines.

Bosses should have identifiable philosophies, reference the climb up their own crew, adapt dialogue dynamically, and speak less frequently but more impactfully.

## Pre-Battle

### Recognition
- "You climbed faster than expected."
- "Everyone in this district knows your car."
- "I watched your races."

### Philosophy

Each boss should embody a racing ideology: precision over aggression, mechanical perfection, fearless risk, psychological warfare, endurance racing, controlled chaos.

- "Street racing isn't about speed. It's about control."
- "Anyone can push throttle. Few can manage pressure."
- "Machines reflect their drivers."

### Stakes
- Big cash payout
- Rare mods unlocked in the shop
- Territory
- Crew respect
- District unlocks
- Narrative reveals

## Mid-Battle Boss Dialogue

Boss dialogue should react to QTE streaks, momentum swings, signature moves, and near collapse.

### Even State
- "Good. Make this memorable."
- "Now I understand the hype."
- "You're forcing me to focus."

### Player Advantage
- "Interesting…"
- "You broke my rhythm."
- "You learned faster than expected."

### Opponent Advantage
- "You still hesitate."
- "That's why you're not ready."
- "You're fighting the car instead of driving it."

### Critical State
- "This is where racers reveal themselves."
- "Push harder."
- "Don't disappoint me now."

# 6. Training Race (Finale training arc)

Approved by Austin 2026-11-13 ("let's push those lines. Always able to change them later."). Written by Nathaniel. Cast and shape: canon 11, `plot.md` Finale "Training arc". Each race has an *Offer* (pre-race), *Mid* (during), *Win* and *Loss/retry* line, and a *Swap* line flags a corner where the trainer drives the next Myth Driver's line and bluff (which corners and how many is Barry's call). Myth Driver names are `<TBD>`.

- **Framing (canon 11):** these are training sessions, not rematches. The crew member offers to train the PC: they heard what the PC is doing, and they respect it.
- **Voice:** same as `barks.md` §3 and §4, but as a coach, not a rival. Lines are short and in-world, with no trash talk about the old loss.
- **Constraints these lines keep:**
  - No trainer names the accusation (canon 2), reveals a Myth Driver's real reason, or explains the sweep (canon 3, 16).
  - The P3 old head stays sincere, still feeds the PC's blame, and reveals nothing.
  - Trainers carry word to the next Myth Driver but never put two Myth Drivers together (canon 10).
  - The PC faces each Myth Driver alone.
- **Myth Driver names are `<TBD>`** (Open Question 2), so these lines say "he" or the Myth Driver's number in brackets.
- **Format:** each race has *Offer* (pre-race), *Mid* (during), *Win* and *Loss/retry*. Loss/retry is used because crew loss persistence applies (canon 27; design/01 line 21).
- **Corner swaps:** the *Swap* line flags a corner where the trainer drives the next Myth Driver's line and bluff. Which corners, and how many, is Barry's.
- No numbers. Behind the screen, lines are POW/EN/HAN. No bark says OUT/MID/IN.
