# Credits — character bases

**Derived from:** "Anime Character Low Poly / PSX" by StephRobertGames (itch.io: stephrobertgames). The licence is **CC0**, and it was free ($0).
https://stephrobertgames.itch.io/anime-character-low-poly-psx

- It served as the starting point and reference: the 1.82 m T-pose proportions, the mitten hands, and the painted-face PS1 approach.
- The bases in this folder were **rebuilt from scratch by script** (`build/`). This covers the mesh, the Mixamo-named skeleton, the weights, the shape keys and all textures. No mesh, rig or texture data from the original was copied.
- The original's **Akira/Kaneda-like look (red jacket and pants, face) is fully replaced**. The replacements are neutral painted outfits (tee + jeans + sneakers, a teal hoodie and a mustard jacket), neutral faces and new hairstyles.
- The original download is **not** included in the repo.

CC0 needs no attribution. This note is kept for provenance.
