# Midnight Apex logo

Approved by Austin on 2026-11-02: round-3 **V3**, a black-and-white one-line wordmark.

- Solid MIDNIGHT with the chicane M, and speed flares on the left.
- A heavily extruded APEX. The A is a racing line: wide entry, a kerb at the clipped apex, wide exit.
- Katakana subtext under APEX (ミッドナイト・エイペックス).
- No blade, and every edge is hard (no round caps or joins).

`final/` has white-on-black and black-on-white versions as SVG and PNG, plus 64px checks. `logo_round3_sheet.jpg` shows the other versions considered. `src/` holds the build scripts: the letter shapes are in `glyphs.py`, and `build_round3.py` imports the round-2 builder. The katakana uses Dela Gothic One, a free OFL font.
