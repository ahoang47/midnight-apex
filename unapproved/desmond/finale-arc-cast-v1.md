# Pre-finale arc: who the 10 races are (draft, Desmond, 2026-11-13)

**Status:** unapproved proposal for Austin. It answers Barry's econ-v1 brief (`finale_arc_recommended.json` in Barry's econ-v1 draft). Design canon is at proto-build 43eb7b4. Nothing live changes until Austin approves.

## Fit with the story

The arc fits, and it fixes a gap: canon says the Myth Drivers each "only agree to race once he hears the one before him did" (story/canon.md 11). The races before each Myth Driver are how word reaches him and how the PC earns the meeting. The arc is not just grind.

- **Pre-F1 (Touge 10/20/20/30): "the road up."** #1's challenge is "come find me up the road. Alone." This comes right after the rift, so the PC is alone for real: no Jesse, tuning at Malo's, full price at Reiko's. The four races are the climb to #1. The opponents are **the old mountain**: drivers from #1's era who, as he says, "never came back after that night". They come back out because word spreads that someone answered #1. Each one has a piece of the night as he remembers it, colored by blame that points at Sabine. That feeds the PC's grudge before F1, so F1's "She left. Nobody pushed her." lands harder.
  - **P4 (30-turn gate):** #1's last old running mate, the one who decides whether the PC is worth #1's time.
  - **P1 grants T4:** see the canon conflict below.
- **F1→F2 (Wangan 10, then a 20 wall): "word travels down to the bay."** #2 hears #1 raced. The wall racer is someone in #2's circle who tests the PC first. That's cheaper to write than #1's old guard, because #2's guilt beat needs room in F2 itself.
- **F2→F3 (Street 10, then a 20 wall):** the same pattern. #3's golden-boy circle closes ranks: the wall racer is someone protecting #3's image (e.g. pro-scene hopefuls who idolize him). That sets up his "Some of us paid for that night."
- **F3→F4 (Touge 10, then a 20 wall):** depends on what F4 is (see the open question below).

## Named vs wild-style

To keep writing and art load sane:
- The **10-turn races are wild-style** (the district's shared wild line and bluff, generic racer type) with a one-line framing bark about why they're out tonight: P1, Wangan 10, Street 10, Touge 10.
- The **20/30-turn races are named characters** with their own corner style (per canon Tracks, named characters drive their own style): P2, P3, P4 and the three 20-turn walls. That's 6 named one-scene characters, each with a short bark set and no sheet-level arc.
- **No Round 1–3 crew members return.** The round structure says each crew is raced once with no rematches, so corner-swapped crew members would break it.
- **Persistence:** I suggest these behave like Myth Driver races (no loss persistence), since they're Finale story races. If Barry wants them on the lieutenant tier for barks, that's fine too.

## Count / placement

- Pre-F1 at 4 is OK story-wise *only* with the "road up" framing. Without it, 4 races stall #1's challenge right after the rift. If the sim allows, 3 (merge P2/P3) reads tighter. Story has no hard objection to 4.
- The two races between each pair of finale bosses are fine. Don't go above 2: the Finale's momentum is the unraveling (canon 16, 18).
