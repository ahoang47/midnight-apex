shots12: order only changes after the Exit QTE (UNAPPROVED)
==========================================================
Austin 2026-11-12: "Order only changes after exit QTE."

What changed
------------
Until the Exit QTE finishes (qte_i < 3), rival_offset holds the pre-turn gap
(off0) exactly and there is no pass_plan. Once qte_i >= 3, off1 is computed
once from the final phase scores via the same Combat.resolve_turn /
Combat.mean_phase_qte path that core.submit_qte uses, the open-lane plan is
sampled on the new post-Exit window, and the gap eases from off0 to off1 over
playback_flow.pass_after_exit_s (smoothstep). core.submit_qte still runs at
the end of playback; RESULT does not snap the gap.

Timing
------
disp.playback_s is 3.6 s of driving clock (qte_t does not advance during a
QTE hold). The Exit hold is at arc end + 6 m, and playback ends at arc end
+ 10 m, so leftover driving after the Exit QTE is only:

  HP  (corner 1): _hold_t(2) = 3.4417 s, remaining 0.1583 s (4.0 m)
  N90 (corner 2): _hold_t(2) = 3.3704 s, remaining 0.2296 s (4.0 m)

That is too short to read a pass (the old window was (pass_u1-pass_u0) *
playback_s = 2.52 s). pass_after_exit_s = 2.5 s (PLACEHOLDER) is that same
readable length, starting when the Exit QTE ends. Because only ~0.16-0.23 s
of playback is left, the lerp continues into the RESULT / Enter-to-continue
phase. Enter and auto-continue wait until the lerp finishes, so the next
turn never starts mid-pass.

s_player still caps at the end of playback, so the cars do not drive off the
exit road and the Exit camera (authored path, u clamped at 1 after playback)
is unchanged. The planned fixed camera hold after the Exit QTE, watching the
car pull away, is not built.

pass_u0 / pass_u1 are unused. The open-lane sampler uses the same
pass_after_exit_s window, with s at 8 + travel * min(1, (_hold_t(2)+t) /
playback_s), so the plan is taken on the exit, which is where the overlap
now happens.
