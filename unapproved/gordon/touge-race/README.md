# Touge race (Gordon [godot], UNAPPROVED, uncommitted)

This is a 10-turn, all-downhill Touge race. It extends the two-corner playtest and is built on Arthur's Cel A soft copy in `unapproved/arthur/experiments/wangan_two_corner/godot`.

**Status (2026-11-19):** the work now lives in the **root Godot project** (`scenes/`, `scripts/`, `shaders/`, `spec/`, textures in `assets/tex/kit/` and `assets/tex/kit/env/`, props in `assets/env/`, cars from `assets/cars/<car>/car_<car>.glb`, tools in `tools/touge_race/`). It is rebased onto origin/touge-race (b9cf654, after the 3748b4f promotion). The copy that used to be in `godot/` here was removed. Nothing is committed.

## What it does

- **Menu → car select.** You pick your car and the rival's car from all five: BRZ, GR Corolla, MX-5, S2000 and WRX STI. Car choice changes the model only.
- **Parked start.** Both cars sit side by side on the start line (±1.75 m), then the game asks "Lead or chase first?". A flat painted white start stripe runs across the road just ahead of the bumpers, and a faded grid box marks each car's slot (Austin t121 item 12). Both are flat paint drawn with the chevron shader, so they are never inked (Cel A road-marking rule). They slide away with the road once the race starts. Sizes: `display.json` `start_marks` (PLACEHOLDER).
- **Track.** 10 turns, from the sim lap `T1_Akina_Switchbacks`: HP ES HP DR ES HP N90 HP DR CH.
  - The road is 7.5 m kerb to kerb (Austin t121 item 10). Lanes are OUT / MID / IN at −1.75 / 0 / +1.75 m, and the chevrons use the Wangan width (1.9 × 0.68 = 1.292 m). Source: `unapproved/arthur/mockups/mountain_districts/README.md` Pass 5, lines 20-21 (and `district_preview.gd:175-176`). The touge road texture (6 m paint) is stretched to 7.5 m, as in Arthur's V5 preview. The grade is −5 % all the way down, with a 6 % bank in every arc.
  - **Each corner's outside has the guardrail and the drop, and its inside has the cut slope** (Austin t121 item 11). The side swaps halfway along the straight between two corners; the swap turn keeps its side until the turnout lot has ended (`course.gd` `drop_at`, runs built in `track_road.gd`). The inside uses Arthur's 法枠 lattice cut slope (`mtn_lattice_slope_g`, 4 m modules at W + 0.7, gain 0.66, from `unapproved/arthur/mockups/mountain_districts`, `_dress_touge_v2`), with cedars on the cut top. Delineators stand every 8 m on both sides. Streamed props pick their side from the course at their current s, so they stay right during the treadmill.
  - Roadside bands and props are clamped to the room the course leaves on each side (`course.gd` `cap_at`, the 2D Voronoi boundary of the centre line). Because of that, the two legs of a hairpin and the centre of a tight arc no longer overlap or fold.
  - A sodium lamp stands every 48 m.
- **Treadmill (Austin t115u/t116u).** During the QTE holds the road surface, verge, gravel, lattice, guardrails, delineators and cedars all scroll in lockstep with the road. The lamps never scroll.
- **Turnout after turn 5 (Austin t114u, t121 item 13).** This is a roadside parking area cut into the inside of the hill. It is its own scene, `scenes/turnout_lot.tscn` (script `scripts/turnout_lot.gd`). In the running game the node path is **`WorldVP/Dressing/CourseDressing/TurnoutLot`**. Its replaceable children are `Floor`, `Curbs`, `StallLines`, `WheelStops`, `BackWall`, `HillTop`, `Lamps`, `Props` and `LotArt`. Any child authored with content in the .tscn is kept, and the placeholder for that layer is skipped. `LotArt` sits on the lot frame: origin at the road edge at the entrance, +X into the lot, +Z along travel, pitched to the grade. Every child of `Lamps` counts as a lamp for the camera guard. The placeholder ships in this pass because Arthur's lot art is on hold. It has an entrance and an exit taper, an island curb between the road and the lot, 2.5 m painted stalls with wheel stops, a back curb, a lattice back wall, two sodium lamps with ground pools and a vending machine. Both cars slow down without stopping and pull in. The leader takes the deep aisle lane and the other car goes past on the near lane. Then they leave by the exit. The road scroll waits while they are in the lot. Turns 1-5 are banked, heat carries over and the gap meter resets.
- **No visible passing.** The rival always stays on its role's side of you, so the gap and the gap meter carry the result.
- **Cameras (Austin t114u).** The two-corner dynamic cameras are restored on all 10 turns. The Entry selection camera and stops are unchanged. Playback uses the authored corner path through the Entry, Apex and Exit QTE holds. After the Exit QTE the camera holds at its last key and watches the car pull away. The turnout has its own fixed lot shot. The chase cam runs between beats, and the look-back works in every race.
- **Camera clearance.** `scripts/cam_guard.gd` checks every camera pose against a model of the kit: the lattice face, the hill, the guardrail box, the verge drop, the lamp posts and arms, and the lot. It locates each pose on the course in 3D, so stop and corner cameras far from the player are judged against the right part of the road. Authored keys that sit in the cut slope move to the valley side. Moves between camera stops run along the course in (s, d, height) rather than on a straight chord, which on a hairpin cut through the hill. Any other pose is pulled in along its line of sight until it clears. A pose on the look-back is lifted instead. The scripted run prints `CLIP_CHECK` with the raw and fixed counts.
- **Heat.** G2 heat (`heat.gd`, a 1:1 port of sim heat.py) is shown on E3B-style gauges with the release notch at 30 %. During planning and the locked playback the gauges show the planned plays. From RESULT on they show the resolved gauges. The scripted run prints `HEAT_CHECK`, which compares drawn fill with sim value every frame and measures HUD pixels.
- **Pick timer and auto-fill (Austin t121 items 8-9).** The line-select timer is `display.json` `pick_timer_s` = 12.0 s (Austin 2026-11-19; no sim/globals constant exists yet, and Barry is settling design/02's 8 vs 12). When it runs out, the unpicked clashes are filled at random among the allowed stances. The draw uses its own `RandomNumberGenerator` seeded from race seed + turn (`race_core.gd` `autofill_seed`), never the sim RNG. Parity and rng draw counts are untouched, and replays store the resulting picks. `tools/touge_race/autofill_check.gd` checks this.
- **Debug: timer off (Austin t119u).** F6 toggles it. It can also be set with `-- --no-timer` or with `display.json` `debug.no_timer`, and it is off by default. It stops the planning / line-select timer and the after-turn continue countdown, while the QTE sweeps still run. The HUD then shows `DEBUG: TIMER OFF (F6)`, the LINE LOCK timer reads OFF and the continue line drops its countdown.
- **Input.** Everything works by touch (InputEventScreenTouch; mouse emulation is off) and the keyboard still works. There is a Web export preset (Compatibility renderer, mobile VRAM compression on).

## Numbers

- `scripts/globals.gd` is generated by `tools/touge_race/gen_globals.py` from `sim/` (pin in the header). The car blocks are read from `unapproved/barry/touge-race-parity/blocks.json` at fd59332 and are still UNAPPROVED.
- Every display number is in `spec/display.json` and marked PLACEHOLDER. That covers turnout (lot geometry, lanes, lot camera), corner_cams ES / DR / CH and `d_scale`, cam_guard, visual_gap.touge, by_code ES / DR / CH, parked_cam, touch_ui, lookback and car_shading.

## Checks

- **Parity:** `python3 tools/touge_race/parity_touge.py [N] [--no-draft-blocks] [--player-car X --opp-car Y]` checks the sim against the Godot headless build bit for bit.
- **Game vs sim:** `python3 tools/touge_race/parity_touge.py --game-result <touge_result.json>` replays the windowed game's own race in the sim. It also checks the gauges after every turn: fill bit for bit, and drawn bars against the sim value.
- **Scripted playtest:** `godot --path . -- --script-inputs res://spec/touge_playtest.json --shots <dir>`. Add `--no-cam-guard` for the raw camera count or `--heat-legacy` for the old gauge source. Both are for before / after stills only.
- **Auto-fill:** `godot --headless --path . -s tools/touge_race/autofill_check.gd` checks that the auto-fill is reproducible per seed, changes with the seed, and never draws from the sim RNG.
- **Camera snapshots:** `--clip-shots <dir>` saves the first frame per camera rig that clips by more than 0.3 m. Run it with and without `--no-cam-guard` for before / after pairs.
- **All of it:** `unapproved/gordon/touge-race/run.sh checks`.
- **Mobile:** `/workspace/playtest/pw/mobile_check_v2.js` runs Playwright mobile emulation against the Web export.
