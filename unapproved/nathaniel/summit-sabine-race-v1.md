## (a) Where racing Sabine at F4 meets existing canon

The race itself doesn't conflict: canon 4 already makes the climax "a final race between the PC and Sabine at the summit of Fushigi-Yama". The friction is in what now surrounds it. All paths are `story/canon.md` unless noted.

1. **The blowup comes first, off the mountain.** Canon 4 (line 22), plot §8 (`plot.md` line 187) and `dialogue_outline.md` line 279 put "I know what really happened." *after* the Finale races and *before* the Summit, and end with "the PC leaves". In the race list, F3 (#3) → the F3→F4 trainer pair → F4. Canon doesn't say whether the blowup comes before or after that pair. Either works, but the Summit race has to follow a scene where Sabine sent the PC away. (The quote is in canon decision **4**, not 1. Decision 1 is "Three Myth Drivers".)
2. **The crews come up vs. Sabine runs the mountain alone and keeps clear of the crews.** Canon 11 (line 56) and `plot.md` line 171 say "the crews come up the mountain" before the Summit. But canon 2 (line 17), canon 14 (line 69) and canon 24 (line 110) say Sabine keeps clear of every crew. The Open Question 8 first pass (canon 4 line 24, `dialogue_outline.md` line 284) has her running "the mountain alone". A crowded mountain is the opposite of how she'd normally be found there.
3. **The accusation never spread.** Canon 2 (line 17): only the three Myth Drivers and Sabine know about the accusation. Canon 3: she has told no one. If the crews are still on the mountain for the reunion, where #2's "You told everyone I asked you out" and the reasons come out (`dialogue_outline.md` lines 287–292), it all goes public. The old flame (canon 21) is private too, and the promoter is a crew boss who may be in the crowd.
4. **The Myth Drivers' first meeting since the breakup.** Canon 10 (line 46) and canon 4 (line 26) say the Summit is their first meeting. Trainers "carry word to the next Myth Driver" (canon 11 line 56). Word passed through trainers is fine, but no trainer scene can put two Myth Drivers in one place before F4.
5. **Sabine learns the reasons only at the Summit, from them.** Canon 3 (line 19), canon 16 (line 80) and canon 4 (line 26) require this. The race has to sit before, around or after that moment, which is Open Question 9 (canon 4 line 25, line 202, `plot.md` §9 line 196). Racing at F4 doesn't settle it, but every option below has to pick a slot.
6. **The rift stands until the final race.** `plot.md` line 235 says "the rift stands until the final race at the summit". Canon 5 (line 28) says she has "the PC's crew now", which needs the PC's apology and repair (Open Question 7). So the race, or the scene around it, has to carry the PC's apology.
7. **The Summit's race tier.** design/01 line 4 lists the tiers as wild, betting, lieutenant, crew boss and Myth Driver race. A race against Sabine fits none of them, so loss persistence (canon 27, line 130; design/01 line 8) isn't defined for F4. This is mechanical and goes to Barry (d).
8. **Sabine has no battle identity.** `story/characters/sabine.md` lines 21–47 are all TODO. She's also the Prologue's race 1 (`dialogue_outline.md` §2), so canon will need to say whether the Summit Sabine drives the same as the Prologue Sabine. She sits outside the racer-type table (canon, Racer Types). For (d).
9. **The epilogue is also on Fushigi-Yama.** Canon 13 (line 67) says the hometown crew comes to Fushigi-Yama for a race just for fun, "possibly with Sabine, Jesse and Reiko watching". If the crews also crowd the mountain at F4, the two scenes could blur. Low risk, but worth keeping apart.
10. **"Alone."** #1's challenge ends on "Alone." (`dialogue_outline.md` line 229), and canon 11 keeps "the PC still faces each Myth Driver alone". Sabine isn't a Myth Driver, so nothing breaks. But whether the PC faces her alone or with an audience is a tone choice tied to 2 and 3.

## (b1) Why Sabine is at the Summit — options for Austin (no pick)

- **Option, for Austin — W1: Reiko points the way (the current first pass).** Reiko sees the PC rounding up the Myth Drivers and says, "She runs the mountain alone some nights. You know where." The PC goes up to her.
  - *Changes:* nothing new. Open Question 8 gets more passes.
  - *Tension:* the crews coming up the mountain (conflict 2).
- **Option, for Austin — W2: she comes because the mountain filled up.** Word spreads that every crew is heading up Fushigi-Yama and that all three Myth Drivers have raced. It's her mountain, and she comes to see who's on it.
  - *Changes:* Open Question 8 gets a new driver, and Reiko's line becomes optional.
  - *Tension:* she'd be walking toward the crews she avoids (canon 2, 14).
- **Option, for Austin — W3: the PC challenges her.** This mirrors the Prologue, where she challenged the PC on the back road (canon 14). The PC sends word, through Malo or Reiko, and she accepts because racing is the one way left to talk. That's tired, not proud (`sabine.md` line 9).
  - *Changes:* Open Question 8 becomes the PC's challenge. Reiko can still say where.
- **Option, for Austin — W4: someone in her circle brings her.** Jesse or Malo talks her into it. That pays off Jesse's arc, where he stops smoothing things over and takes a side (Open Question 12).
  - *Changes:* Jesse's or Malo's beat moves into the Summit setup.
  - *Risk:* it takes some agency from the PC.

*Observation:* W1 and W3 keep Sabine's "alone" thread and don't need her to walk into the crowd. W2 and W4 need conflict 2 handled.

## (b2) How the race fits the blowup, the reunion and the ending — options for Austin (no pick)

All of these keep the race at F4. They differ on where it falls relative to the blowup, the reunion and the apologies (Open Question 9).

- **Option, for Austin — R1: race first, reunion after.**
  - Order: the blowup off the mountain stays as written. At the top she won't talk ("You want to say something, say it on the road"), and the race is how the PC earns the conversation.
  - After the race, the Myth Drivers step out, it's their first meeting (10), and she hears their reasons (3, 16). Then come the PC's apology, the offer, her refusal and the parting shot (5).
  - *Canon 4:* the "I know what really happened" blowup stays as written. *Reunion:* happens after the race. *Ending:* matches canon 5's "after the final race and the apology party".
  - *Changes:* Open Question 9 resolves to "after". `plot.md` §9 gets the order.
- **Option, for Austin — R2: reunion first, race last.**
  - Order: the Myth Drivers and the PC apologize at the top, and she hears the reasons. Then she settles it the only way that feels honest to her, with a race, and the refusal and parting shot come after.
  - *Canon 4:* the blowup stays as written. *Reunion:* happens before the race. *Ending:* canon 5's order still holds, since the ending comes after both.
  - *Changes:* Open Question 9 resolves to "before".
  - *Note:* the race becomes the emotional release rather than the climax of tension. That echoes the epilogue's "Talk's cheap. Race us." (canon 13), so the two beats would need to feel different.
- **Option, for Austin — R3: the race carries the anger.**
  - Order: the race takes over part of the blowup, in driving form. She drives angry, and her barks carry the "second wound" (canon 4, 15). It ends with the reasons at the top, from the Myth Drivers.
  - *Sub-choice:* the off-mountain "I know what really happened." scene stays as the trigger, or it moves to the summit, where the PC says it before the race.
  - *Changes:* if it moves, canon 4 line 22, `plot.md` §8 and the `dialogue_outline.md` blowup section change location. Her race barks need to be written either way.
  - *Constraint:* she must not learn the reasons mid-race from the PC (canon 3, 16), so her barks are anger, not discovery.
- **Option, for Austin — R4: the reasons come out mid-race.**
  - The Myth Drivers are posted on the climb and their lines land during the race. This is the most cinematic, but it splits the reunion into barks.
  - *Changes:* canon 4 line 26 and canon 10 (their first meeting) need clarifying, since they would be "meeting" across a race, not face to face. The `dialogue_outline.md` reunion lines (287–292) become mid-race lines.

*Observation:* R1 and R2 need the fewest changes, only an answer to Open Question 9. R3 is close if the blowup stays off the mountain. R4 changes the most.

**The crowd, which applies to any option (option, for Austin):** the crews can watch the race and then head down before the reunion, or be gone before it starts. That keeps canon 2 ("never spread") and canon 21 (the old flame) private. *Observation:* if the crowd stays for the reunion, canon 2 has to change.
