# Trainer barks for the training arc (draft v1, Nathaniel, 2026-11-13)

**Status:** unapproved. **Waiting on Austin's OK of the wording.** Austin approves bark text himself before it moves into `story/barks.md`.

- **Cast and shape:** canon 11 (`story/canon.md` line 56) and `story/plot.md` lines 167–172 (proto-build 68a1ecb).
- **Framing (canon 11):** these are training sessions, not rematches. The crew member offers to train the PC: they heard what the PC is doing, and they respect it.
- **Voice:** same as `barks.md` §3 and §4, but as a coach, not a rival. Lines are short and in-world, with no trash talk about the old loss.
- **Constraints these lines keep:**
  - No trainer names the accusation (canon 2), reveals a Myth Driver's real reason, or explains the sweep (canon 3, 16).
  - The P3 old head stays sincere, still feeds the PC's blame, and reveals nothing.
  - Trainers carry word to the next Myth Driver but never put two Myth Drivers together (canon 10).
  - The PC faces each Myth Driver alone.
- **Myth Driver names are `<TBD>`** (Open Question 2), so these lines say "he" or the Myth Driver's number in brackets.
- **Format:** each race has *Offer* (pre-race), *Mid* (during), *Win* and *Loss/retry*. Loss/retry is used because crew loss persistence applies (canon 27; design/01 line 21).
- **Corner swaps:** the *Swap* line flags a corner where the trainer drives the next Myth Driver's line and bluff. Which corners, and how many, is Barry's.
- No numbers. Behind the screen, lines are POW/EN/HAN. No bark says OUT/MID/IN.

## Before #1: the road up the mountain (Touge)

### P1: Touge kid (10; the win unlocks the big finale mods)
- **Offer:** "Heard he called you out. Up here you don't answer that cold. Run with me first."

- **Mid:** "See? You're braking where he wants you to."

### P2: Touge mature member (20)
- **Offer:** "The kid showed you three corners. The mountain's twenty."
- **Mid:** "You're driving it like a sprint. Save something for the second lap."
- **Swap:** "He doesn't change his line. That's the whole trick. Stop waiting for him to."
- **Win:** "That's the long game. Now you're starting to drive like you're from here."

### P3: the Touge old head who saw Sabine on the phone (20, wall)
*Sincere, still feeds the PC's blame, reveals nothing. He doesn't bring up the phone call again; the PC already has it.*

- **Offer:** "I'm not here to talk about her. I said what I saw."
- **Mid:** "Old line. Nobody runs it anymore. He still does."
- **Swap:** "Decreasing radius. He commits early and never lets go. Make him pay for it."
- **Win:** "Good. Go get your answers. Somebody should."
- **Loss/retry:** "Not yet. You hesitated where I told you."

### P4: Touge crew boss (30, gate; knew #1 back then)
- **Offer:** "He told you I used to be hard to reach? He'd know. He's the one who climbed past me."
- **Offer:** "I ran with him before any of you knew his name. Let's see if you're ready."
- **Mid:** "He'll pick his line and dare you to move him. Don't try to move him. Beat him out of it."
- **Swap:** "This one's his. Every time, since before you were driving."
- **Win:** "You're ready. Go up the road. He'll be there. Alone, like he said."
- **Loss/retry:** "Not ready. Come back when you stop flinching on the esses."

*#1's sighting line "Used to be a lot harder to reach `<Touge crew boss>`" is paid off by the boss's first Offer line. The exact echo is for Austin.*
