# Pre-finale arc: who the 10 races are (draft v1.3, Desmond, 2026-11-13)

**Status:** approved by Austin 2026-11-13 ("Training arc sounds fine."), folded into canon at 68a1ecb. Canon in design/01 wins over this note.

## The idea

After the rift, Sabine's circle closes on the PC: Jesse shuts them out and Reiko goes back to full price. What the PC still has is the respect they earned on the street. Crew members the PC beat in Rounds 1–3 hear about #1's challenge (and, later, each Myth Driver race) and come back to **train** the PC. These are training sessions, not rematches: the crew member offers, and the framing is "let me show you what he does." The PC still faces each Myth Driver alone, so #1's "Alone." holds.

The trainers also carry canon's "each Myth Driver only agrees to race once he hears the one before him did": the district's crew knows where to find their Myth Driver and passes the word.
