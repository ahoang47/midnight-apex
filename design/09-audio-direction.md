# 09 — Audio Direction

> Owner: Desmond (Chief of Design) until an audio owner is assigned. Status: **working direction, not final.** Austin decides changes.

## Music direction (working)

Austin's current thinking for the Midnight Apex soundtrack blends three genres:

- **Jazz fusion**
- **Industrial house**
- **Drum and bass**

This is a starting direction for mood and reference gathering, not a locked style. Nothing here assigns genres to specific scenes, districts, or race phases yet.

## Open questions

- Which genre (or blend) goes with which context: overworld/night city, garage and shop, story scenes, races, boss and Myth Driver races.
- Whether districts (Touge, Wangan, Urban Street) get their own musical identity.
- Adaptive music: whether race tracks react to clash results, HP or race phase.
- Production: Austin works in Ableton Live; stem and file format conventions for Godot import are TBD.
